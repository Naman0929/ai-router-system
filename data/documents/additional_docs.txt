LEAD MANAGEMENT GUIDE

Lead Status Definitions:

NEW - Recently acquired leads that haven't been contacted
- Typical source: web forms, referrals, marketing campaigns
- Action required: Initial outreach within 24 hours
- Success metric: Contact rate within SLA

CONTACTED - Leads that have been reached but not yet qualified
- Includes phone calls, emails, or other communication attempts
- May require multiple touch points
- Track: response rate, engagement level

QUALIFIED - Leads that meet our ideal customer profile
- Budget confirmed ($1,000+ monthly spend)
- Authority: decision maker or strong influence
- Need: clear use case for our product
- Timeline: implementation within 6 months

NURTURING - Qualified leads not ready to purchase immediately
- Typically 3-12 month buying timeline
- Require regular follow-up and value-add content
- Track: engagement with nurture campaigns

OPPORTUNITY - Active sales conversations in progress
- Proposal sent or demo completed
- Clear next steps and timeline defined
- Probability scoring applied

CLOSED_WON - Successfully converted to paying customer
- Contract signed and implementation begun
- Handoff to customer success team
- Track: deal size, sales cycle length

CLOSED_LOST - Leads that will not convert
- Document reason: competitor, budget, timing, no need
- Consider for future re-engagement campaigns
- Maintain relationship for referral opportunities

Lead Scoring System:

Demographic Scoring (max 40 points):
- Company size: 500+ employees (10 pts), 100-499 (7 pts), 50-99 (5 pts), <50 (2 pts)
- Industry match: High priority (10 pts), Medium (7 pts), Low (3 pts)  
- Geographic location: Primary markets (10 pts), Secondary (7 pts), Other (2 pts)
- Job title: C-level/VP (10 pts), Director (8 pts), Manager (5 pts), Other (2 pts)

Behavioral Scoring (max 60 points):
- Website visits: >10 pages (15 pts), 5-10 (10 pts), 3-5 (5 pts), 1-2 (2 pts)
- Content downloads: >3 pieces (15 pts), 2-3 (10 pts), 1 (5 pts)
- Email engagement: High (15 pts), Medium (10 pts), Low (3 pts)
- Event attendance: Webinar (10 pts), Demo request (15 pts), Conference (5 pts)

Total Score Interpretation:
- 80-100: Hot lead - immediate follow-up
- 60-79: Warm lead - contact within 48 hours  
- 40-59: Cold lead - nurture campaign
- <40: Unqualified - consider disqualification

SALES PROCESS WORKFLOW:

Stage 1: Lead Qualification (Goal: 24-48 hours)
1. Review lead source and initial information
2. Conduct qualification call using BANT framework
3. Update lead status and score in CRM
4. Schedule follow-up or move to nurturing

Stage 2: Needs Discovery (Goal: 1-2 weeks)
1. Conduct detailed discovery call
2. Document specific use cases and requirements
3. Identify decision makers and buying process
4. Provide initial solution overview

Stage 3: Solution Presentation (Goal: 2-3 weeks)
1. Prepare customized demo/proposal
2. Present solution addressing specific needs
3. Handle objections and gather feedback
4. Establish next steps and timeline

Stage 4: Negotiation and Closing (Goal: 2-4 weeks)
1. Present pricing and contract terms
2. Address final concerns and objections
3. Negotiate terms within approved parameters
4. Secure signature and begin implementation

CRM USAGE GUIDELINES:

Required Fields for All Leads:
- Contact information (name, email, phone, company)
- Lead source and campaign attribution
- Lead status and last activity date
- Lead score (updated automatically)
- Assigned sales representative

Activity Logging Requirements:
- All customer interactions must be logged within 24 hours
- Include outcome, next steps, and follow-up date
- Use standard activity types for reporting consistency
- Tag activities with relevant campaign or initiative

Reporting and Analytics:
- Weekly pipeline review meetings
- Monthly lead quality analysis
- Quarterly conversion rate optimization
- Annual lead source ROI evaluation

Data Quality Standards:
- Duplicate detection and merging procedures
- Lead routing rules based on geography and company size
- Data enrichment through third-party services
- Regular data cleanup and validation processes
